WHY BUY THROUGH VNR

The paperwork gets checked before the plot gets sold.

Title

Every parcel is verified for a 30-year encumbrance-free chain before it's added to a layout — not after a buyer asks.

Approvals

HMDA and RERA numbers are issued before marketing begins, so financing and resale are never held up later.

Infrastructure

Roads, drainage and electricity are laid as part of development — not promised as a "future phase."

Handover

You get physical possession, registration documents and a layout map on the same visit.

FROM VISIT TO REGISTRATION

How a purchase actually moves.

  1. Site visit

    Walk the layout, see the plot markers in person, and get the approved master plan on the spot.

  2. Document check

    We share the title chain, HMDA/RERA certificates and layout approval before you commit any money.

  3. Booking

    A token payment holds your plot while the sale agreement is drawn up and reviewed.

  4. Registration

    We coordinate the sub-registrar appointment and handle the paperwork alongside you, start to finish.
